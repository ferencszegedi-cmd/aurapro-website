# FELÚT+ — "There is a way up"

**Concept note for a transnational consortium**
Target call: **ESF-SI-2026-ROMA NEETs-01** — Supporting access to the labour
market for Roma NEETs · Deadline: 30 September 2026

---

## In one sentence

FELÚT+ tests whether a trusted community voice — one of Hungary's most
recognised Roma artists — can bring hard-to-reach Roma NEETs through the door
of employment services that have failed to reach them for years, and then move
them along a structured pathway into work, education or training.

## The problem we address

Across Hungary, Slovakia and Romania, Roma young people are dramatically
over-represented among NEETs. Activation programmes exist — but a large share
of the target group never enters them. The barrier is rarely a lack of
information; it is **a lack of trust in institutions**, reinforced by years of
exclusion and by outreach channels (leaflets, job-centre letters, school
announcements) that this group has learned to ignore.

Conventional outreach spends money reaching people who are already reachable.

## Our proposition: trust as outreach infrastructure

**L.L. Junior** is a Roma-born Hungarian artist, founding member of the
pioneering Roma hip-hop group Fekete Vonat, with 25+ years at the top of the
Hungarian music scene, ~500,000 YouTube subscribers and 600M+ cumulative views.
He grew up in the same circumstances as the target group and speaks their
language — literally and culturally. Crucially, **his audience extends across
the borders**: Hungarian-speaking Roma communities in southern Slovakia
(Gemer), the Romanian Partium and beyond share the same language and the same
cultural references.

FELÚT+ turns this reach into an activation instrument:

1. **20 roadshow events** across three countries (HU 10, SK 5, RO 5) —
   music, plus the ambassador telling his own story: where he started, where he
   got to, and what nearly stopped him (drugs, usury, dropping out of school).
2. **The FELÚT desk on site.** A Roma case manager, a public employment
   service officer and a local education partner register interested young
   people *at the event itself*. No follow-up letter, no waiting period — the
   moment of motivation becomes an enrolment.
3. **An individual pathway** follows: assessment → Roma mentor → skills
   development (including a financial self-defence module against predatory
   lending) → job placement with employer partners → 6-month follow-up.
4. **A prevention track** for 14–18 year-olds still in school, linked to local
   after-school programmes, to stop the next cohort from becoming NEETs.

## What makes it innovative

- **A measurable model of celebrity-led outreach.** Community icons are
  routinely used for awareness campaigns; they are almost never integrated into
  a service pathway with measured conversion. FELÚT+ measures exactly that:
  how many attendees convert to enrolment, training, and employment.
- **Zero-latency conversion.** The gap between raised interest and actual
  enrolment is where most campaigns lose their audience. We remove the gap.
- **A cross-border natural experiment.** Hungarian-speaking Roma communities
  in three countries share language and culture but live under three different
  policy regimes. Running the same intervention across all three produces
  directly comparable evidence on what is method-driven and what is
  system-driven — rare and policy-relevant knowledge.

|---|---|

> On My Way Kft., the production company through which we would enter the

> ambassador's engagement and cross-border reach, event production and video

### The ambassador services package

The ambassador line is a **contracted service package with defined
deliverables**, not an appearance fee:

| Deliverable | Volume |
|---|---|
| Live appearances (music block + motivational session) | 20 |
| Professionally produced video summaries of the events | 20 |
| Distribution on the ambassador's own channels (~500,000 subscribers) | 20 releases |
| Campaign face: image and name rights across all project communication | 24 months |
| Press and broadcast appearances as project spokesperson | min. 8 |
| Participation in opening and closing conferences | 2 |

Valued at advertising rates, the projected 5M+ views alone approach the value
of this budget line.

## What we bring to the table

- A fully developed programme concept, methodology and event format
- The ambassador's commitment and his cross-border reach
- Event production, video documentation and communication capacity
- A mapped venue network across Hungary, Slovakia and Romania — cultural
  centres and community houses in Roma communities, with local co-hosts
- A domestic pilot (12 events) planned from national funding, which can serve
  as documented proof of concept

## What we are looking for

A **coordinator** eligible under the call conditions and able to carry the 20%
co-financing, plus national partners in Slovakia and Romania. We are open to
adapting the design to the coordinator's priorities — the model, not the
ownership, is what matters to us.

If carrying the full co-financing is not feasible at this project volume, we
would rather know at the outset than at the drafting stage. The design scales
down: the call's lower grant threshold is EUR 800,000, where the co-financing
requirement falls to EUR 200,000. We are willing to discuss a smaller first
edition on that basis.
